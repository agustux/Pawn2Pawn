Pawn2Pawn is a chess game for two players on nearby Android phones. Devices connect directly over Wi-Fi Direct: no internet connection, no server, no account, no tracking.

Features:
• Find nearby players and invite them with a tap
• Full chess rules: castling, en passant, promotion, check, checkmate and stalemate

Requires a device with Wi-Fi Direct support. Android 9 or newer.
