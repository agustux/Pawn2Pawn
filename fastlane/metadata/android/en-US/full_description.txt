Pawn2Pawn is a chess game for two players on nearby Android phones. Devices connect directly over Wi-Fi Direct or Bluetooth: no internet connection, no server, no account, no tracking. Connections are encrypted.

Features:
• Find nearby players and invite them with a tap
• Choose Wi-Fi Direct or Bluetooth
• Full chess rules: castling, en passant, promotion, check, checkmate and stalemate

Permissions:
• Network and Wi-Fi: open the direct connection between the two phones (the internet permission is only used for these local sockets)
• Nearby devices / Bluetooth: find and connect to the other player
• Location (Android 12L and older only): required by Android for Wi-Fi Direct and Bluetooth scanning; your location is never used

Requires Wi-Fi Direct or Bluetooth. Android 6 or newer.
